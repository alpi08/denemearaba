04 / METHOD

Four steps. No theatre.

Every engagement begins by understanding the system, rather than selecting a fashionable security deliverable.

We map the actual attack surface, trust boundaries, dependencies and business-critical flows.

We deliberately challenge permissions, assumptions, authentication boundaries and architectural shortcuts.

Findings become engineering changes with measurable ownership and validation criteria.

We retest the important paths and leave the team with evidence instead of decorative paperwork.
