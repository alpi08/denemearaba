Four steps. No theatre.

Every engagement starts with understanding the system, not selecting a fashionable security deliverable.

We map the real attack surface, trust boundaries, dependencies and business-critical flows.

We deliberately try to violate assumptions, permissions and architectural boundaries.

Findings become engineering changes with clear ownership, validation criteria and measurable impact.

We retest the important paths and leave the team with evidence rather than a decorative PDF.
